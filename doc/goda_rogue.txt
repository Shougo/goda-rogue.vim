INTRODUCTION                                          *goda-rogue-introduction*

goda-rogue.vim is a mystery-dungeon roguelike in Vim and Neovim.  It looks
like rogue: the map is drawn with full-width characters, enemies are kanji,
and their rank is their color.  The game is Go, in a binary built from this
plugin, and Vim talks to it through goda.vim.

It is a prototype: it shows the dungeon and a mini-map, the player moves
around and attacks, and the enemies wander, chase and attack the player.
Going down the stairs of the 30th floor clears the dungeon.  Items can be
picked up and dropped, and a weapon, a shield and a bracelet equipped
(weapons add to the attack, shields make the defense, a bracelet has its
effect while it is worn).  Grasses are drunk, scrolls read, staffs waved
(a magic bolt to the front) and food eaten, arrows shot, and any item
thrown.  Pots hold items: a pot of keeping to take them out again, a pot
of synthesis merges them (a weapon or a shield gets the seals and the
upgrade value of another of its kind put in after it; a bracelet is
mended by the same bracelet).  A thrown pot breaks, and what was in it
falls around.

Weapons and shields can have seals (印), one kanji each, shown in the
inventory (鉄の剣+2【会必】): they work while the weapon or the shield is
equipped, as many as it has room for.

Grasses, scrolls, staffs, bracelets and pots are unidentified at first:
they go by names such as 赤い草 (with a "?" in the inventory), different
in each run.  Using one, or wearing a bracelet, tells what its kind is; a
scroll of identification tells it too, and also the upgrade value of a
weapon or a shield (shown once it is equipped too) and the charges of a
staff.

						*goda-rogue-floors*
Some floors are special: one big room; a maze of corridors, with things
anywhere in it; and a monster house, a room full of sleeping monsters
and items, who all wake up when the player comes in.  In a big room, the
house is the whole floor: its monsters wake up one by one, and when the
player comes near.

						*goda-rogue-shops*
Some floors have a shop: a room of items for sale, with their prices,
and shopkeepers (商, in the color of their rank, reversed) by its doors.
Picking an item up puts it on the bill: a shopkeeper then stands in its
door, and walking into one asks to pay.  An item put down in the shop
can be sold for half its price.  Leaving the shop without paying (by
another door, a warp, a pit) is stealing: the shopkeepers get angry, and
guards come after the thief.  The stronger the shopkeepers (their rank,
their number), the harder it is; deep shops sell rarer items.  Attacking
a shopkeeper makes them angry too; with no shopkeeper left, the items are
anyone's.

						*goda-rogue-traps*
Traps are hidden on the floors.  A swing at nothing (|goda-rogue-attack|)
finds a trap in front of the player, or shows the tile as safe, as
walking on it does; stepping on a hidden trap sets it off: a pit (down to
the next floor), a landmine (half the HP), a snare (no moving for some
turns), a poison arrow (damage, and the strength down), sleeping gas,
warping, hunger, rust (the upgrade values of the weapon and the shield
down).  A trap the player has found shows as 罠, a dash stops before it,
and by the rule of the built-in dungeon it never goes off.

The player gets hungry: the fullness (満腹, on the top line) goes down a
point every few turns.  Food restores it.  Starving, the player restores
no HP and loses a point every turn, until it eats or dies.

Every action that takes a turn (moving, attacking, waiting, a step of a
dash) is followed by the turn of the monsters.  Slow monsters act every
other turn, fast ones twice a turn.  Some monsters doze until the player
enters or leaves their room, or they are attacked.  Walking and waiting
restore HP; at 0 HP the run is over.

Defeating an enemy gives its EXP to the player and to every ally, whole:
they level up on the same table.  At the end of a run, the result screen
shows how it ended, the floor, the level, the enemies defeated, the
turns, the play time and the seed, and under it the floor as it was at
the end, with the last messages, to see what happened.

Lingering on a floor does not pay: now and then a monster appears out of
sight, and it gives nothing for defeating it.  Then Kron's wind blows:
the first time as a warning (the status line shows the turns until the
next one), the second time a strong monster comes for the player and
every enemy grows one rank stronger, the third time the run is over.

==============================================================================
INSTALL                                                    *goda-rogue-install*

It needs Vim 9.1 or Neovim 0.11, Go 1.26 and goda.vim:

https://github.com/Shougo/goda.vim

The binary is built on the first start, into a cache directory: "goda-rogue"
under |stdpath()| "cache" in Neovim, $XDG_CACHE_HOME/vim or ~/.cache/vim in
Vim.  Build it again with |goda_rogue#build()|.

EXAMPLES                                                  *goda-rogue-examples*

There are no default mappings: map the actions in the game buffer. >vim

	autocmd FileType goda-rogue call s:goda_rogue_settings()
	function s:goda_rogue_settings() abort
	  const dirs = #{h: 'left', j: 'down', k: 'up', l: 'right',
	        \ y: 'upleft', u: 'upright', b: 'downleft', n: 'downright'}
	  for [key, dir] in items(dirs)
	    execute $'nnoremap <buffer> {key}'
	          \ $'<Cmd>call goda_rogue#do("move", "{dir}")<CR>'
	    execute $'nnoremap <buffer> g{key}'
	          \ $'<Cmd>call goda_rogue#do("turn", "{dir}")<CR>'
	    execute $'nnoremap <buffer> {key->toupper()}'
	          \ $'<Cmd>call goda_rogue#do("dash", "{dir}")<CR>'
	  endfor
	  nnoremap <buffer> <Space> <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('attack')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> t <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('turn')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> . <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('wait')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> > <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('stairs')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> i <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('inventory')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> , <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('pickup')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> f <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('shoot')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> x <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('look')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> m <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('map')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> M <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('log')<CR>
	endfunction

	call goda_rogue#start()
<

FUNCTIONS                                                *goda-rogue-functions*

                                                        *goda_rogue#start()*
goda_rogue#start([{options}])
		Start a new game in the current window, in the buffer
		"goda-rogue://game" (filetype "goda-rogue").
		{options} is a |Dictionary|:
		  dungeon	The ID of the dungeon (see
				|goda_rogue#dungeons()|).
				Default: ask which, with the
				selection UI (|goda-rogue-prompts|).
		  seed		A string or a number.  The same seed
				makes the same floors in the same
				dungeon, whatever the player does on
				them.  Default: a random one, shown in
				the status line.

                                                     *goda_rogue#dungeons()*
goda_rogue#dungeons()
		The dungeons there are: a |List| of |Dictionary| with
		"id", "name", "description" and "floors".

                                                           *goda_rogue#do()*
goda_rogue#do({action} [, {arg}...])
		Run the action {action} of the game (see
		|goda-rogue-actions|) and show the screen after it.  It does
		nothing outside the game buffer.  An error is printed.

                                                        *goda_rogue#build()*
goda_rogue#build()
		Build the binary again, after an update of this plugin.  The
		running one is stopped; the next action starts the new one.

ACTIONS                                                    *goda-rogue-actions*

The directions are "left", "right", "up", "down", "upleft", "upright",
"downleft" and "downright".

move {direction}					*goda-rogue-move*
		Face {direction} and step there.  Diagonal steps around a
		corner of a wall are not possible.  Stepping onto an ally
		changes places with it.  Toward an enemy, the player only
		faces it: attacking is |goda-rogue-attack|.

dash {direction}					*goda-rogue-dash*
		Step to {direction} until something stops the player: a
		wall, a monster, an item or the stairs under it, or entering
		or leaving a room.

attack							*goda-rogue-attack*
		Attack the tile the player faces.  It costs a turn, even
		when nothing is there, but allies are not attacked.  There is
		no attack around a corner of a wall.  A swing at nothing
		checks the tile: it finds a trap there, or shows the tile
		as safe (|goda-rogue-traps|).

shoot							*goda-rogue-shoot*
		Shoot an arrow of those equipped to the front.  It flies up
		to 10 tiles, until it hits a monster (and is used up) or a
		wall (and falls there).  It costs a turn; without arrows
		equipped, nothing happens.

turn [{direction}]					*goda-rogue-turn*
		Face {direction}.  Without it, face an enemy next to the
		player that it can attack: again and again, each of them in
		turn, clockwise.  It costs no turn.

wait							*goda-rogue-wait*
		Let a turn pass (and restore HP).

stairs							*goda-rogue-stairs*
		Go down the stairs the player stands on.  An ally next to
		the player comes along.

pickup							*goda-rogue-pickup*
		Pick up the item under the player.  Walking onto an item
		picks it up; a dash only stops on it.  Money is not carried:
		it adds to the gold of the top line.

inventory						*goda-rogue-inventory*
		Choose an item of the inventory, and then what to do with
		it (|goda-rogue-prompts|): 飲む (a grass), 読む (a scroll),
		振る (a staff), 食べる (food), 装備する or 外す (a weapon, a
		shield, a bracelet or arrows), 投げる (throw it to the front,
		as an arrow flies: a grass has its effect on the monster it
		hits, other items hurt it a little and fall by it), 入れる
		or 出す (put an item in a pot, take one out), 置く (drop
		it).  The items are sorted by kind,
		each with the glyph of its kind as an icon.

choose {index}						*goda-rogue-choose*
cancel							*goda-rogue-cancel*
		Answer the prompt the game asks for: the choice {index},
		from 0, or none.  The selection UI does it; any other
		action drops the prompt.

look							*goda-rogue-look*
		Choose one of the monsters and items the player sees, the
		nearest first, with where it is ("右5 上2"), to read about
		it: the rank and the speed of a monster, whether it is
		asleep, the level and HP of an ally.  It costs no turn.

map							*goda-rogue-map*
		Show or hide the mini-map (|goda-rogue-mini-map|).  It costs
		no turn.

log							*goda-rogue-log*
		Show all the messages in the buffer "goda-rogue://log", in a
		window under the game.  It costs no turn.

redraw							*goda-rogue-redraw*
		Show the screen again.  Done for every window that shows the
		game when it is resized.

VARIABLES                                                *goda-rogue-variables*

                                                     *g:goda_rogue#binary*
g:goda_rogue#binary
		The binary to start, instead of the one built in the cache
		directory.  It is not built then: build it yourself.

                                                *g:goda_rogue#prompt_func*
g:goda_rogue#prompt_func
		A function to show the prompts with instead of the
		editor's selection UI (|goda-rogue-prompts|): it gets the
		prompt (a |Dictionary| with "title" and "choices") and a
		function to call with the choice, from 1 (0 to cancel).

HIGHLIGHTS                                              *goda-rogue-highlights*

Define them yourself to change them.  By default, each is linked to the
group of the same name with "Default" appended (GodaRogueWallDefault, ...),
whose colors follow 'background'.

GodaRoguePlayer		The player.
GodaRogueWall		Walls.
GodaRogueFloor		The floor of rooms and corridors.
GodaRogueFloorSafe	The floor known to have no trap: where the player
			has walked.
GodaRogueStairs		The stairs.
GodaRogueTrap		A trap the player has found.
GodaRogueFacing		The tile the player faces: its background, over the
			highlight of what is there.
GodaRogueOldMessage	The messages of the actions before the last one.
GodaRogueTitle		The title of the result screen.
GodaRogueHP		The HP bar of the top line (a background): its
GodaRogueHPLow		filled part, the filled part at a quarter of HP or
GodaRogueHPEmpty	less, and the rest.
GodaRogueRank1		Enemies of rank 1 to 4: green, blue, purple, red.
...
GodaRogueRank4
GodaRogueAlly1		Allies: the color of their rank, underlined.
...
GodaRogueAlly4
GodaRogueKeeper1	Shopkeepers: the color of their rank, reversed.
...
GodaRogueKeeper4

Items are not highlighted.

==============================================================================
SCREEN                                                      *goda-rogue-screen*

Every tile is one full-width character, two cells wide:

	主	the player
	＃	a wall
	．	the floor of a room or a corridor; in another color
		(|GodaRogueFloorSafe|) where the player has walked, which
		is known to have no trap
	段	the stairs
	罠	a trap the player has found (|GodaRogueTrap|)
	剣 盾 腕 草 巻 杖 壺 矢 金 食
		items
	鼠 蛇 狼 骸 霊 鬼 竜 盗
		enemies (their color is their rank)

The background of the tile the player faces (and attacks) is
|GodaRogueFacing|.

The top line shows what matters all the time: the floor, the level, HP
with a bar, the fullness, and the gold.  Then the window shows the part of the floor
around the player that fits.  Under the map are the status line (the
attack, the defense and the strength, the turn and the turns on this
floor, the steps, the play time (as of the last action), the direction
the player faces as an arrow, the seed, and the turns until the next
wind once it has blown) and the last three messages, those of the
actions before the last one dimmed (|GodaRogueOldMessage|).  The result
screen shows the turns, the steps and the play time too.

==============================================================================
PROMPTS                                                    *goda-rogue-prompts*

When the game asks for a choice (which item, what to do with it), it is
shown with the editor's selection UI: |popup_menu()| in Vim,
|inputlist()| in Neovim, or vim.ui.select() once a plugin replaces it
there.  Set |g:goda_rogue#prompt_func| to show it another way.

Lists of items show the icon, "E" for the equipped ones, and the name, in
columns: >
	 4: 剣 E 会心剣【会】
	 5: 盾   木の盾
<

DUNGEONS                                                  *goda-rogue-dungeons*

A dungeon is data: a directory of tab-separated text files, in
"rogue/dungeons/{id}/" of this plugin for the built-in ones.  Lines that
start with "#" are comments.  Change them and build the binary again
(|goda_rogue#build()|) to change the dungeon.  The same seed makes other
floors once the files change.

	header.txt		The name (名前), the description (説明) and
				the number of floors (階数): key<TAB>value.
	rules.txt		The rules: the player at the start, HP
				regeneration, fullness, spawning, Kron's wind, combat
				constants, the growth table of the player,
				the first ally, whether a trap the player
				has found goes off (見つけた罠: 安全 or 発動):
				key<TAB>value.  A later line
				overrides an earlier one.
	monsters.txt		The monsters, a table.  A family is a glyph
				(字), a kind of it a rank (ランク, 1 to 4).
				種別 is - for monsters of the floors, 店主
				for shopkeepers, 番犬 for the guards after
				a thief: these never appear by themselves,
				nor rank up.
	monster_table.txt	Which monsters appear on which floors (開始
				to 終了), by weight (頻度), a table.
	items.txt		The items, a table.  種類 is the kind of an
				item, which decides what it can do: 武器 盾
				腕輪 矢 草 巻物 杖 壺 食料 お金.  強さ is
				the strength of a weapon or a shield, and
				how much the effect of another item does.
				効果 is what an item does (- for nothing),
				by its kind (the comments of the built-in
				items.txt tell what each does):
				  腕輪: ちから 回復 気配察知 道具感知
				  草: HP回復 ちから
				  巻物: あかり 識別
				  杖: 吹き飛ばし
				  食料: 満腹
				  壺: 保存 合成
				The 強さ of arrows is their attack.  回数 is
				how many times a staff can be waved, how
				many arrows there are, how many items a pot
				holds (min-max; - for other items).  印 is
				the seal a weapon or a shield is born with
				(- for none), 印数 how many seals it can
				have.  ひび is how easily a
				bracelet cracks, a percent (0 for never).
				Leaving a floor where a bracelet had its
				effect (it showed a monster out of sight,
				restored HP, added to an attack), it cracks
				with ひび x (cracks + 1) % (times
				腕輪の傷みやすさ, a percent, in rules.txt:
				100 as items.txt says, 0 never, 200 twice
				as easily), and one crack
				more than ひびの段階 (rules.txt) breaks it.
				The cracks are shown in the inventory
				([ひび1]).  値段 is the price in shops (of
				one arrow); a shop buys for half of it.
	item_table.txt		Which items appear on which floors, a table.
	seals.txt		The seals of weapons and shields, a table:
				印 is the kanji of a seal, 効果 what it does
				(the comments of the built-in seals.txt tell
				what each does), 強さ how much.
	fake_names.txt		The names of unidentified items, by kind
				(種類), a table.  The items of a kind with
				names are unidentified at first, each with
				one of the names, chosen by the seed; a kind
				needs a name for each of its items.
	traps.txt		The traps, a table: 効果 is what a trap does
				(the comments of the built-in traps.txt tell
				what each does), 強さ how much.
	trap_table.txt		Which traps appear on which floors, a table.
	shops.txt		The shops, a table: 品 the kinds of items
				it sells (- for any), 店主 the shopkeeper
				and 店主の数 how many, 番犬 the guard after
				a thief and 番犬の数 how many.
	shop_table.txt		Which shops appear on which floors, a table.
	shop_item_table.txt	Which items the shops sell on which floors,
				a table: 店 is a shop, or - for any shop
				that sells the kind of the item.
	floors.txt		How many monsters, items and traps (罠の数,
				optional) a floor starts with (min-max), how
				much of it the player sees (明るさ,
				optional): 通常 the room it is in and around
				it, 明るい all of the floor (as a scroll of
				light does), 暗い only around it; and the
				chances (percent, optional) of a floor of
				one big room (大部屋), of a maze (迷路), of
				a monster house (モンスターハウス), and of a
				shop (店), a table.
	growth.txt		The growth tables: the total EXP for each
				level from 2, and the HP, attack and defense
				a level adds, a table.

A table starts with a line of column names, in any order; the files of
"cave" show them.  Mistakes are reported with their file and line: glyphs
must be one full-width character, IDs must exist, every floor needs its
monsters, items and counts, levels go in order.

MINI-MAP                                                  *goda-rogue-mini-map*

The part of the floor the player has seen, reduced to one half-width
character a tile, in a popup window (Vim) or a floating window (Neovim).  It
goes to the corner of the game window away from the player, and is hidden
while the game buffer is not shown.

	@	the player
	.	the floor of a room or a corridor (walls are left out)
	>	the stairs
	*	an item
	o	a monster the player sees (the color of its rank) or an
		ally (underlined)
