INTRODUCTION                                         *goda-rogue-introduction*

goda-rogue.vim is a mystery-dungeon roguelike in Vim and Neovim.  It looks
like rogue: the map is drawn with full-width characters, enemies are kanji,
and their rank is their color.  The game is Go, in a binary built from this
plugin, and Vim talks to it through goda.vim.

It is a prototype: it shows the dungeon and a mini-map, the player moves
around and attacks, and the enemies wander, chase and attack the player.
Going down the stairs of the 30th floor clears the dungeon.  Items can be
picked up and dropped, and a weapon, a shield and a bracelet equipped
(weapons add to the attack, shields make the defense, a bracelet has its
effect while it is worn).  Grasses are drunk, scrolls read, staffs waved
(a magic bolt to the front) and food eaten, arrows shot, and any item
thrown.  Pots hold items: a pot of keeping to take them out again, a pot
of synthesis merges them (a weapon or a shield gets the seals and the
upgrade value of another of its kind put in after it; a bracelet is
mended by the same bracelet).  A thrown pot breaks, and what was in it
falls around.

Weapons and shields can have seals (印), one kanji each, shown in the
inventory (鉄の剣+2【会必】): they work while the weapon or the shield is
equipped, as many as it has room for.

Grasses, scrolls, staffs, bracelets and pots are unidentified at first:
they go by names such as 赤い草 (with a "?" in the inventory), different
in each run.  Using one tells what its kind is; a bracelet tells it when
its effect shows while it is worn (at once for one that senses, at the
first blow for one of strength).  A scroll of identification tells it
too, and also the upgrade value of a weapon or a shield (shown once it
is equipped too; until then the weapon or the shield has a "?": 木刀?)
and the charges of a staff.

                                                           *goda-rogue-floors*
Some floors are special: one big room; a maze of corridors, with things
anywhere in it; and a monster house, a room full of sleeping monsters
and items, who all wake up when the player comes in.  In a big room, the
house is the whole floor: its monsters wake up one by one, and when the
player comes near.

                                                            *goda-rogue-shops*
Some floors have a shop: a room of items for sale, with their prices,
and shopkeepers (商, in the color of their rank, reversed) by its doors.
Picking an item up puts it on the bill: a shopkeeper then stands in its
door, and walking into one asks to pay.  An item put down in the shop
can be sold for half its price.  Leaving the shop without paying (by
another door, a warp, a pit) is stealing: the shopkeepers get angry, and
guards come after the thief.  The stronger the shopkeepers (their rank,
their number), the harder it is; deep shops sell rarer items.  Attacking
a shopkeeper is stealing too.  Angry shopkeepers sell no more, and with
none left either: the items are anyone's.  Items not paid for cannot be
put in a pot.
In a shop that trades, the inventory shows what the shop pays for each
item (売), and the description of an item shows its price; for an item
not identified, it also shows the kinds it may be by that price (the
kinds of its category of the same price, not identified yet), as a player
who knows the prices would reckon.

                                                            *goda-rogue-traps*
Traps are hidden on the floors, more in a monster house, never at the
entrance of a room (its tiles next to a corridor).  A swing at nothing
(|goda-rogue-attack|) finds a trap in front of the player, or shows the
tile as safe, as walking on it does; stepping on a hidden trap sets it
off: a pit (down to the next floor), a landmine (half the HP), a snare
(no moving for some turns), a poison arrow (damage, and the strength
down), sleeping gas, warping, hunger, rust (the upgrade values of the
weapon and the shield down).  A trap the player has found shows as 罠,
a dash stops before it, and by the rule of the built-in dungeon it never
goes off by itself: standing on it, the inventory lists it (marked
[足元]), to step on it on purpose (踏む).  |goda-rogue-look| tells its kind and what it does.

The player gets hungry: the fullness (満腹, on the top line) goes down a
point every few turns.  Food restores it.  Starving, the player restores
no HP and loses a point every turn, until it eats or dies.

Every action that takes a turn (moving, attacking, waiting, a step of a
dash) is followed by the turn of the monsters.  Slow monsters act every
other turn, fast ones twice a turn.  Some monsters doze until the player
enters or leaves their room, or they are attacked.  Walking and waiting
restore HP; at 0 HP the run is over.

Defeating an enemy gives its EXP to the player and to every ally, whole:
they level up on the same table.  At the end of a run, the result screen
shows how it ended, the floor, the level, the enemies defeated, the
turns, the play time and the seed, and under it the floor as it was at
the end, with the last messages, to see what happened.

Lingering on a floor does not pay: now and then a monster appears out of
sight, and it gives nothing for defeating it.  Then Kron's wind blows:
the first time as a warning (the status line shows the turns until the
next one), the second time a strong monster comes for the player and
every enemy grows one rank stronger, the third time the run is over.

==============================================================================
INSTALL                                                   *goda-rogue-install*

It needs Vim 9.1 or Neovim 0.11, Go 1.26 and goda.vim:

https://github.com/Shougo/goda.vim

The binary is built on the first start, into a cache directory: "goda-rogue"
under |stdpath()| "cache" in Neovim, $XDG_CACHE_HOME/vim or ~/.cache/vim in
Vim.  Build it again with |goda_rogue#build()|.

EXAMPLES                                                 *goda-rogue-examples*

There are no default mappings: map the actions in the game buffer. >vim

	autocmd FileType goda-rogue call s:goda_rogue_settings()
	function s:goda_rogue_settings() abort
	  const dirs = #{h: 'left', j: 'down', k: 'up', l: 'right',
	        \ y: 'upleft', u: 'upright', b: 'downleft', n: 'downright'}
	  for [key, dir] in items(dirs)
	    execute $'nnoremap <buffer> {key}'
	          \ $'<Cmd>call goda_rogue#do("move", "{dir}")<CR>'
	    execute $'nnoremap <buffer> g{key}'
	          \ $'<Cmd>call goda_rogue#do("turn", "{dir}")<CR>'
	    execute $'nnoremap <buffer> {key->toupper()}'
	          \ $'<Cmd>call goda_rogue#do("dash", "{dir}")<CR>'
	  endfor
	  nnoremap <buffer> <Space> <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('attack')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> t <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('turn')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> . <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('wait')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> > <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('stairs')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> i <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('inventory')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> , <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('pickup')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> f <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('shoot')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> x <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('look')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> m <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('map')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> I <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('items')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> M <Cmd>call goda_rogue#do('log')<CR>
	  nnoremap <buffer> S <Cmd>call goda_rogue#suspend()<CR>
	endfunction

	call goda_rogue#start()
<

FUNCTIONS                                               *goda-rogue-functions*

                                                          *goda_rogue#start()*
goda_rogue#start([{options}])
		Start a new game in the current window, in the buffer
		"goda-rogue://game" (filetype "goda-rogue").
		{options} is a |Dictionary|:
		  dungeon	The ID of the dungeon (see
				|goda_rogue#dungeons()|).
				Default: ask which, with the
				selection UI (|goda-rogue-prompts|).
		  seed		A string or a number.  The same seed
				makes the same floors in the same
				dungeon, whatever the player does on
				them.  Default: a random one, shown in
				the status line.
		  debug		|v:true| enables the debug mode
				(|goda-rogue-debug|).  Default:
				|g:goda_rogue#debug|.
		A suspended run (|goda-rogue-replays|) is offered to be
		resumed first.  Called while a run is played, it starts
		another one: suspend it first to keep it.

                                                       *goda_rogue#dungeons()*
goda_rogue#dungeons()
		The dungeons there are: a |List| of |Dictionary| with
		"id", "name", "description" and "floors".

                                                             *goda_rogue#do()*
goda_rogue#do({action} [, {arg}...])
		Run the action {action} of the game (see
		|goda-rogue-actions|) and show the screen after it.  It does
		nothing outside the game buffer.  An error is printed.

                                                        *goda_rogue#suspend()*
goda_rogue#suspend()
		Suspend the run: it is kept, and |goda_rogue#start()| offers
		to resume it, even in another session of the editor.  The
		same as the action "suspend".

                                                    *goda_rogue#save_replay()*
goda_rogue#save_replay([{name}])
		Save the replay of the last run as {name} (".replay" is
		added; default: the date and time) in the directory
		"replays" of |g:goda_rogue#data_dir|, and return its path.

                                                         *goda_rogue#replay()*
goda_rogue#replay([{file}])
		Watch the replay {file} (default: the last run) in the
		game buffer: it plays by itself, an action every
		|g:goda_rogue#replay_interval| milliseconds, with how far it
		is under the screen.  The actions of the game do nothing
		meanwhile, but "map" and "log".

                                                   *goda_rogue#replay_pause()*
goda_rogue#replay_pause()
		Pause the replay being watched, or play it again.

                                                    *goda_rogue#replay_step()*
goda_rogue#replay_step([{n}])
		Step the replay being watched {n} actions (default 1; -1:
		to its end).

                                                        *goda_rogue#records()*
goda_rogue#records()
		Show the records of the finished runs (番付) in the buffer
		"goda-rogue://records": the best run of each dungeon (a
		clear before a fall, the fastest clear, the deepest fall;
		runs that used the debug mode never count), then every run,
		the newest first.  Each run that ends adds its line to
		"records.tsv" in |g:goda_rogue#data_dir| (tab-separated:
		date, dungeon, seed, floor, cleared, result, turns, play
		time in milliseconds, debug).  Records are only a record:
		nothing of them goes into a run.

                                                  *goda_rogue#last_replay()*
goda_rogue#last_replay()
		The path of the replay file of the last run.

                                                          *goda_rogue#build()*
goda_rogue#build()
		Build the binary again, after an update of this plugin.  The
		running one is stopped; the next action starts the new one.

ACTIONS                                                   *goda-rogue-actions*

The directions are "left", "right", "up", "down", "upleft", "upright",
"downleft" and "downright".

move {direction}                                             *goda-rogue-move*
		Face {direction} and step there.  Diagonal steps around a
		corner of a wall are not possible.  Stepping onto an ally
		changes places with it.  Toward an enemy, the player only
		faces it: attacking is |goda-rogue-attack|.

dash {direction}                                             *goda-rogue-dash*
		Step to {direction} until something stops the player: a
		wall, a monster, an item or the stairs under it, or entering
		or leaving a room.

attack                                                     *goda-rogue-attack*
		Attack the tile the player faces.  It costs a turn, even
		when nothing is there, but allies are not attacked.  There is
		no attack around a corner of a wall.  A swing at nothing
		checks the tile: it finds a trap there, or shows the tile
		as safe (|goda-rogue-traps|).

shoot                                                       *goda-rogue-shoot*
		Shoot an arrow of those equipped to the front.  It flies up
		to 10 tiles, until it hits a monster (and is used up) or a
		wall (and falls there).  It costs a turn; without arrows
		equipped, nothing happens.

turn [{direction}]                                           *goda-rogue-turn*
		Face {direction}.  Without it, face an enemy next to the
		player that it can attack: again and again, each of them in
		turn, clockwise.  It costs no turn.  Until the next action,
		the line the player faces is highlighted to the first wall
		(GodaRogueFacing): the axis of a throw, an arrow or a staff.

wait                                                         *goda-rogue-wait*
		Let a turn pass (and restore HP).

stairs                                                     *goda-rogue-stairs*
		Go down the stairs the player stands on.  An ally next to
		the player comes along.

pickup                                                     *goda-rogue-pickup*
		Pick up the item under the player.  Walking onto an item
		picks it up; a dash only stops on it.  Money walked onto
		adds to the gold of the top line (the purse); money a dash
		stopped on is under the player, to pick up: it is carried
		then, as an item, to throw (|goda-rogue-inventory|).

inventory                                               *goda-rogue-inventory*
		Choose an item of the inventory, and then what to do with
		it (|goda-rogue-prompts|): 飲む (a grass), 読む (a scroll),
		振る (a staff), 食べる (food), 装備する or 外す (a weapon, a
		shield, a bracelet or arrows), 投げる (throw it to the front,
		as an arrow flies: a grass has its effect on the monster it
		hits, money deals its amount divided by お金投げ of
		rules.txt, whatever the defense, and is used up, other
		items hurt it a little and fall by it), 財布に入れる (put
		money in the purse, the gold; no turn), 見る or 入れる (see
		what is in a pot, put an item in it), 置く (drop it), 説明
		(what it is and does: the attack of a weapon, its seals,
		the effect of a grass; no turn).  The items are sorted by
		kind, each with the glyph of its kind as an icon; a pot
		shows the icons of what is in it (保存の壺[1]【草草巻】).
		The item under the player comes last, marked [足元]: it can
		be picked up (拾う; money: 財布に入れる too), swapped with
		an item carried (交換: one turn, also offered on the items
		carried; not in a shop), used or thrown where it lies.  A
		scroll of identification can identify it too.  A found trap
		under the player is listed too: 踏む sets it off on purpose.
		見る lists the items in a pot: one of them can be taken out
		(出す), and in a pot of keeping (保存の壺) also used or
		thrown where it is.  A pot of synthesis (合成の壺) gives
		nothing back: a weapon or a shield put in it first gets
		the seals and upgrades of another of its kind.  A
		material (an item with a seal, as a grass) is not
		merged in it: mixed synthesis (異種合成) is a monster's,
		as in Shiren.

choose {index}                                             *goda-rogue-choose*
cancel                                                     *goda-rogue-cancel*
		Answer the prompt the game asks for: the choice {index},
		from 0, or none.  The selection UI does it; any other
		action drops the prompt.

look                                                         *goda-rogue-look*
		Choose one of the monsters, items and found traps the
		player sees, the nearest first, with where it is ("右5
		上2"), to read about it: the rank and the speed of a
		monster, whether it is asleep, the level and HP of an ally;
		the kind of a trap and what it does.  It costs no turn.

map                                                           *goda-rogue-map*
		Show or hide the mini-map (|goda-rogue-mini-map|).  It costs
		no turn.

items                                                       *goda-rogue-items*
		Show or hide the items carried (|goda-rogue-item-list|), to
		keep them in sight.  It costs no turn.

log                                                           *goda-rogue-log*
		Show all the messages in the buffer "goda-rogue://log", in a
		window under the game.  It costs no turn.

redraw                                                     *goda-rogue-redraw*
		Show the screen again.  Done for every window that shows the
		game when it is resized.

suspend                                                   *goda-rogue-suspend*
		Suspend the run (|goda_rogue#suspend()|).

debug [{command} [{arg}]]                                   *goda-rogue-debug*
		The debug mode, to test the game or a dungeon quickly: it
		works only when the run was started with it
		(|g:goda_rogue#debug|) or the dungeon allows it (デバッグ
		in rules.txt, |goda-rogue-dungeons|).  Without {command}, it
		asks which, with the selection UI.  {command}:
		  floor {n}	Go to the floor {n}.
		  item {id}	Give an item (its ID in items.txt).
		  monster {id}	Put a monster near the player.
		  reveal	Show the whole floor.
		  identify	Identify every item.
		  invincible	The player cannot fall (again: it can).
		  gold		Give 1000G.
		It costs no turn.  A run that used it is marked "デバッグ"
		on its status line, its result and its summary, and its
		replay replays the debug actions too.

VARIABLES                                               *goda-rogue-variables*

                                                         *g:goda_rogue#binary*
g:goda_rogue#binary
		The binary to start, instead of the one built in the cache
		directory.  It is not built then: build it yourself.

                                                          *g:goda_rogue#debug*
g:goda_rogue#debug
		|v:true| enables the debug mode (|goda-rogue-debug|) in the
		runs started from now on.  Default: |v:false|.

                                                       *g:goda_rogue#data_dir*
g:goda_rogue#data_dir
		The directory of the replay file of the last run
		("last.replay"), of the saved replays ("replays"), of the
		replays of the runs before ("replays/auto",
		|g:goda_rogue#kept_replays|) and of the records
		(|goda_rogue#records()|, "records.tsv").
		Default: "goda-rogue" in |stdpath()| "data" (Neovim), or in
		$XDG_DATA_HOME/vim, $LOCALAPPDATA/vim (Windows) or
		~/.local/share/vim (Vim).

                                                 *g:goda_rogue#kept_replays*
g:goda_rogue#kept_replays
		How many replays of the runs before are kept.  When a new
		run replaces the replay file of the last run, that one is
		moved to "replays/auto" in |g:goda_rogue#data_dir|, named
		by when the run began ("20261118-162100-cave.replay"),
		whatever ended it: the end of the run, a suspended run
		not resumed, a bug, the editor killed.  The oldest are
		removed past this number; 0 keeps none.  Default: 50.

                                                *g:goda_rogue#replay_interval*
g:goda_rogue#replay_interval
		The milliseconds between two actions of a replay watched
		(|goda_rogue#replay()|).  Default: 300.

                                                    *g:goda_rogue#prompt_func*
g:goda_rogue#prompt_func
		A function to show the prompts with instead of the
		editor's selection UI (|goda-rogue-prompts|): it gets the
		prompt (a |Dictionary| with "title" and "choices") and a
		function to call with the choice, from 1 (0 to cancel).

HIGHLIGHTS                                             *goda-rogue-highlights*

Define them yourself to change them.  By default, each is linked to the
group of the same name with "Default" appended (GodaRogueWallDefault, ...),
whose colors follow 'background'.

GodaRoguePlayer		The player.
GodaRogueWall		Walls.
GodaRogueFloor		The floor of rooms and corridors.
GodaRogueFloorSafe	The floor known to have no trap: where the player
			has walked.
GodaRogueStairs		The stairs.
GodaRogueTrap		A trap the player has found.
GodaRogueFacing		The tile the player faces: its background, over the
			highlight of what is there.  After turning, the
			whole line the player faces (|goda-rogue-turn|).
GodaRogueOldMessage	The messages of the actions before the last one.
GodaRogueTitle		The title of the result screen.
GodaRogueHP		The HP bar of the top line (a background): its
GodaRogueHPLow		filled part, the filled part at a quarter of HP or
GodaRogueHPEmpty	less, and the rest.
GodaRogueRank1		Enemies of rank 1 to 4: green, blue, purple, red.
...
GodaRogueRank4
GodaRogueAlly1		Allies: the color of their rank, underlined.
...
GodaRogueAlly4
GodaRogueKeeper1	Shopkeepers: the color of their rank, reversed.
...
GodaRogueKeeper4

Items are not highlighted.

SCREEN                                                     *goda-rogue-screen*

Every tile is one full-width character, two cells wide:

	主	the player
	＃	a wall
	．	the floor of a room or a corridor; in another color
		("GodaRogueFloorSafe") where the player has walked, which
		is known to have no trap
	段	the stairs
	罠	a trap the player has found ("GodaRogueTrap")
	剣 盾 腕 草 巻 杖 壺 矢 金 食
		items
	鼠 蛇 狼 霊 盗 骸 鬼 蟹 蜂 蛙 茸 竜 熊 亀 猪 鎧
		enemies (their color is their rank); in each rank, the
		first five are weak, the next six middle, the last five
		strong

The background of the tile the player faces (and attacks) is
"GodaRogueFacing".

The top line shows what matters all the time: the floor, the level, HP
with a bar, the fullness, and the gold.  Then the window shows the part of the floor
around the player that fits.  Under the map are the status line (the
attack, the defense and the strength, the turn and the turns on this
floor, the steps, the play time (as of the last action), the direction
the player faces as an arrow, the seed, and the turns until the next
wind once it has blown) and the last three messages, those of the
actions before the last one dimmed ("GodaRogueOldMessage").  The result
screen shows the turns, the steps and the play time too.

PROMPTS                                                   *goda-rogue-prompts*

When the game asks for a choice (which item, what to do with it), it is
shown with the editor's selection UI: |popup_menu()| in Vim,
|inputlist()| in Neovim, or vim.ui.select() once a plugin replaces it
there.  Set |g:goda_rogue#prompt_func| to show it another way.

Lists of items show the icon, "E" for the equipped ones, and the name, in
columns: >
	 4: 剣 E 会心剣【会】
	 5: 盾   木の盾
<

DUNGEONS                                                 *goda-rogue-dungeons*

A dungeon is data: a directory of tab-separated text files, in
"rogue/dungeons/{id}/" of this plugin for the built-in ones.  Lines that
start with "#" are comments.  Change them and build the binary again
(|goda_rogue#build()|) to change the dungeon.  The same seed makes other
floors once the files change.

	header.txt		The name (名前), the description (説明) and
				the number of floors (階数): key<TAB>value.
	rules.txt		The rules: the player at the start, HP
				regeneration, fullness, spawning, Kron's wind,
				combat constants, the growth table of the
				player, the first ally, whether a trap the
				player has found goes off (見つけた罠: 安全 or
				発動), and, optional, whether runs may use
				the debug mode (デバッグ: 可 or 不可, the
				default; |goda-rogue-debug|), for the makers
				of a dungeon, and what money thrown deals
				(お金投げ: its amount divided by it, default
				10): key<TAB>value.  A later line
				overrides an earlier one.
	monsters.txt		The monsters, a table.  A family is a glyph
				(字), a kind of it a rank (ランク, 1 to 4).
				速度 is 鈍足 (every other turn), 通常, 倍速
				(twice a turn) or 移動倍速 (twice a turn,
				but one attack at most).
				種別 is - for monsters of the floors, 店主
				for shopkeepers, 番犬 for the guards after
				a thief: these never appear by themselves,
				nor rank up.  能力, optional, is its special
				abilities, separated by spaces (- for
				none), each with its numbers after colons:
				  怒り:H:A   at H% of its max HP or below,
				             its attack becomes A% (once)
				  痛恨:C:D   C% of its blows are critical,
				             doing D% of the damage
				  ふらふら:C  C% of its actions are a step
				             in a random direction
				  突進:R:D   with the player on one of its
				             lines, 2 to R tiles away, it
				             rushes up, does D damage to the
				             first creature in the way and
				             blows it away (as the staff)
				  引き寄せ:R:C:H  with the player on one
				             of its lines, 2 to R tiles away
				             (R 0: anywhere in its room),
				             C% of its actions pull the
				             player next to it, and then it
				             hits (H 1) or not (0)
				眠り, optional, is the share of the kind
				asleep on a new floor, a percent (- for a
				third).
	monster_table.txt	Which monsters appear on which floors (開始
				to 終了), by weight (頻度), a table.
	items.txt		The items, a table.  種類 is the kind of an
				item, which decides what it can do: 武器 盾
				腕輪 矢 草 巻物 杖 壺 食料 お金.  強さ is
				the strength of a weapon or a shield, and
				how much the effect of another item does.
				効果 is what an item does (- for nothing),
				by its kind (the comments of the built-in
				items.txt tell what each does):
				  腕輪: ちから 回復 気配察知 道具感知
				  草: HP回復 ちから
				  巻物: あかり 識別
				  杖: 吹き飛ばし
				  食料: 満腹
				  壺: 保存 合成
				The 強さ of arrows is their attack.  回数 is
				how many times a staff can be waved, how
				many arrows there are, how many items a pot
				holds (min-max; - for other items).  印 is
				the seal a weapon or a shield is born with
				(- for none), 印数 how many seals it can
				have.  On another item, 印 makes it a
				material: merged into a weapon or a shield
				by mixed synthesis (異種合成, by a monster;
				a pot of synthesis merges items of a kind
				only), it gives its seal for weapons or for
				shields, and is used up.  ひび is how easily a
				bracelet cracks, a percent (0 for never).
				Leaving a floor where a bracelet had its
				effect (it showed a monster out of sight,
				restored HP, added to an attack), it cracks
				with ひび x (cracks + 1) % (times
				腕輪の傷みやすさ, a percent, in rules.txt:
				100 as items.txt says, 0 never, 200 twice
				as easily), and one crack
				more than ひびの段階 (rules.txt) breaks it.
				The cracks are shown in the inventory
				([ひび1]).  値段 is the price in shops (of
				one arrow); a shop buys for half of it.
	category_tables.txt	The item tables, a table: 表 is the name of
				a table, 種類 a kind of items and 頻度 its
				weight on the floors 開始 to 終了.  A table
				picks an item in two stages: first its kind
				by weight, then an item of that kind by
				weight (item_tables.txt), so adding items of
				a kind does not crowd out the other kinds.
				The floors (floors.txt 道具表) and the shops
				(shops.txt 品表) name their table.
	item_tables.txt		The items of the item tables, a table: 表,
				the item (ID) and its weight (頻度) among
				the items of its kind, on the floors 開始 to
				終了.  Every kind a table gives on a floor
				needs an item there, and every item a kind
				with a weight.
	guaranteed_items.txt	Items that surely appear (optional), a
				table: each row puts the item (ID) on one
				floor of 開始 to 終了, the seed decides
				which, on top of the items of the floor.
	seals.txt		The seals of weapons and shields, a table:
				印 is the kanji of a seal, 効果 what it does
				(the comments of the built-in seals.txt tell
				what each does), 強さ how much, 付く先 what it
				goes on (武器 or 盾, optional: either).  Give
				the seals of weapons and of shields different
				effects: the seals of the two add up.  A
				kanji may have two rows, one for weapons and
				one for shields: the seal of a material is
				the one for what it is merged into.
	fake_names.txt		The names of unidentified items, by kind
				(種類), a table.  The items of a kind with
				names are unidentified at first, each with
				one of the names, chosen by the seed; a kind
				needs a name for each of its items.
	traps.txt		The traps, a table: 効果 is what a trap does
				(the comments of the built-in traps.txt tell
				what each does), 強さ how much.
	trap_table.txt		Which traps appear on which floors, a table.
	shops.txt		The shops, a table: 品表 the item table it
				sells from (no money in it), 店主 the
				shopkeeper and 店主の数 how many, 番犬 the
				guard after a thief and 番犬の数 how many.
	shop_table.txt		Which shops appear on which floors, a table.
	floors.txt		How many monsters, items and traps (罠の数,
				optional) a floor starts with (min-max), the
				item table of its items (道具表, optional:
				床 by default), how much of it the player
				sees (明るさ, optional): 通常 the room it is
				in and around it, 明るい all of the floor
				(as a scroll of light does), 暗い only around
				it; and the chances (percent, optional) of a
				floor of one big room (大部屋), of a maze
				(迷路), of a monster house (モンスターハウス),
				and of a shop (店); the traps a monster
				house adds in its room (ハウスの罠, optional,
				min-max), a table.
	growth.txt		The growth tables: the total EXP for each
				level from 2, and the HP, attack and defense
				a level adds, a table.

A table starts with a line of column names, in any order; the files of
"cave" show them.  Mistakes are reported with their file and line: glyphs
must be one full-width character, IDs must exist, every floor needs its
monsters, items and counts, levels go in order.

To see how a dungeon plays, let bots play it, from this repository: >
	go run ./cmd/analyze -dungeon cave -runs 200
<	Bots play the runs through the actions, like a player, with what a
player knows: a careful one (it explores, rests, and leaves when the
wind comes, when its food would not last two floors, or when it sees an
enemy it would likely lose to) and a reckless one (it goes down as soon
as it finds the stairs).  Both shoot arrows, and answer enemies next to
them that would kill them: they reckon the chance of dying in the fight
(by the hit rates, the spread of the damage and the speeds of the kinds,
as a player who knows the kinds would) and answer it from 5% (careful)
or 20% (reckless) by the cheapest answer that is enough (by the prices
of the items, cheaper the more of a kind they carry and the deeper they
are; a staff's last wave costs the most): the stairs, a step back into
a corridor to fight two enemies one by one, a healing grass, a staff,
walking away to the stairs.  They shoot arrows at enemies worth them (any with ten arrows or
more), read unknown scrolls on the stairs, check for traps before
stepping in a monster house whose monsters sleep, and the careful one
leaves a floor whose fights cost more answers than it gave.  Out of a fight they heal when their HP is below what the
monsters of the floor could take in two turns (the careful one rests in
a room, not in a corridor), and they wait for an
enemy coming at them rather than step next to it, to strike first.
It prints how many cleared, where and why they fell (by monster, trap,
hunger or the wind; deaths from full HP in one action or just after
arriving count as unfair), the HP on arriving on each floor, the turns
spent on it and the answers (grasses, scrolls, staffs) picked up and used
on it, the items picked up and used, the items left at a clear and at a
death, the chance of dying the bot reckoned at the start of its last fight
and at its last choice (0: a surprise), and the items used on the last
floors before a death.  The runs are the same for
the same seeds (-seed), so a change of the data shows in the numbers.
The bots are simple: they measure, they do not play well.

==============================================================================
MINI-MAP                                                 *goda-rogue-mini-map*

The part of the floor the player has seen, reduced to one half-width
character a tile, in a popup window (Vim) or a floating window (Neovim).  It
goes to the corner of the game window away from the player, and is hidden
while the game buffer is not shown.

	@	the player
	.	the floor of a room or a corridor (walls are left out)
	>	the stairs
	*	an item
	o	a monster the player sees (the color of its rank) or an
		ally (underlined)

The windows beside the game leave the top line, the status line and the
messages uncovered.

==============================================================================
ITEM LIST                                               *goda-rogue-item-list*

The items carried, as the inventory lists them (|goda-rogue-inventory|), in
a popup window (Vim) or a floating window (Neovim) beside the game: shown or
hidden by the action "items", and kept up to date as the game goes, so that
the inventory need not be opened to see them.  Its title is how many items
are carried, of how many ("持ち物 3/20").  The item under the player comes
last, marked [足元], and the found trap there.

It goes to the side of the game window away from the player: in the corner
away from it, or in the other one with the mini-map shown.  It is hidden
while the game buffer is not shown.

==============================================================================
REPLAYS                                                   *goda-rogue-replays*

A run is all in its replay file: the dungeon, the seed, and every action of
the player with its play time, written as the run goes (a crash of the
editor loses nothing).  Replaying the actions makes the same run, since all
the randomness comes from the seed.

The same file is the suspended run: |goda_rogue#start()| offers to resume
the last run when it is not over, whether it was suspended or the editor
was left without suspending.  It is resumed by replaying it, and the play
time goes on from where it was.  A new run replaces it.

A replay is checked as it is replayed: after each action, the file has what
happened in it (lines "# turn event": floors, damage, kills, levels, items
picked up, used or identified, the end), and replaying must make the same.
Another version of the game, or of the data of the dungeon, may not: such a
run cannot be resumed nor watched with it.  |goda_rogue#start()| offers to
save it with |goda_rogue#save_replay()|, to watch it with the version that
played it.

At the end of a run, the result screen has it in a line, to share (the
dungeon, the floor and how it ended, the time, the turns and the seed).
Save the replay with |goda_rogue#save_replay()| to keep it, to share it, or
to attach it to a bug report: it reproduces the run.  A replay holds no
personal data.  The last runs are kept by themselves too, for a while: when
a run went wrong, its replay is in "replays/auto"
(|g:goda_rogue#kept_replays|).
