# The seals (印) of weapons and shields: one kanji each. 効果 is what it
# does while its weapon or shield is equipped. 強さ is how much as the
# ability an item is born with (items.txt 印: 素の能力), 印の強さ as a
# seal merged into another (weaker; optional, 強さ if missing):
#   攻撃      adds it to the attack
#   防御      adds it to the defense
#   会心      its % of the attacks of the player do twice the damage
#   必中      its % of the attacks of the player that would miss hit
#   反撃      with a chance of its %, the damage the player takes goes
#             back to the attacker
#   サビよけ  with a chance of its %, the weapon and the shield do not
#             rust
#   ハラヘラズ  the player gets hungry its % less fast
#   三方向    a blow also strikes the two tiles beside the one the player
#             faces, ahead of it, for its % of the damage
#   盗みよけ  with a chance of its %, nothing is stolen
#   見切り    the blows of the monsters hit its % less often
#   身躱し    with a chance of its %, an item thrown or shot at the player
#             (arrows too; not a piercing arrow, nor what a mirror sends
#             back) misses it and falls at its feet (a trap there goes
#             off)
# The same seal may be there more than once (and with the ability the
# item is born with): 攻撃 and 防御 add up, the others add up as chances
# do (two of 50% make 75%), never past 100%.
# 付く先 is what it goes on, a weapon (武器) or a shield (盾): the seals of
# weapons and of shields have different effects, so the two never add up.
# A seal may have two rows, one for weapons and one for shields: the seal
# of a material (items.txt) is the one for what it is merged into.
