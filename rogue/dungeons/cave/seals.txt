# The seals (印) of weapons and shields: one kanji each. 効果 is what a
# seal does while its weapon or shield is equipped, with 強さ as how much:
#   攻撃      adds 強さ to the attack
#   防御      adds 強さ to the defense
#   会心      強さ % of the attacks of the player do twice the damage
#   必中      the attacks of the player always hit
#   反撃      with a chance of 強さ %, the damage the player takes goes
#             back to the attacker
#   サビよけ  the weapon and the shield do not rust
#   ハラヘラズ  the player gets hungry half as fast
#   ドレイン  a blow gives 強さ % of its damage back as HP (1 at least)
#   三方向    a blow also strikes the two tiles beside the one the player
#             faces, ahead of it
#   盗みよけ  nothing is stolen from the player
#   見切り    the blows of the monsters hit 強さ % less often
# 付く先 is what it goes on, a weapon (武器) or a shield (盾): the seals of
# weapons and of shields have different effects, so the two never add up.
# A seal may have two rows, one for weapons and one for shields: the seal
# of a material (items.txt) is the one for what it is merged into.
