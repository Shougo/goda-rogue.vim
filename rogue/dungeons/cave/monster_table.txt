# The families are of three classes in each rank: weak (鼠 蛇 狼 霊 盗),
# middle (骸 鬼 蟹 蜂 蛙 茸) and strong (竜 熊 亀 猪 鎧). Each class of each
# rank fades in over a few floors and out after its own, so that the
# monsters grow stronger floor by floor, without a floor where they all
# rank up at once: middle ones of rank 1 from 4F, strong ones from 9F
# (all there by 12F), rank 2 from 7F (middle ones from 14F), and strong
# ones of rank 2 from 21F to the end, as many as the middle ones from 24F:
# the last floors are the hardest, above all for a low level. The fast
# 山犬 waits for 3F. The charging 猪 comes later than the other strong
# ones: rank 1 from 15F, rank 2 only on 27-30F, and fewer. The archer 射
# shoots from afar: rank 1 on 6-18F, rank 2 from 22F. The mage 魔 waves
# its staff from afar: rank 1 on 5-16F, rank 2 from 19F. The mimic 化
# hides as an item, found out when picked up: rank 1 on 4-14F, rank 2
# from 15F.
# The mud 泥 splits when hit: rank 1 on 6-16F; rank 2, which also rots
# onigiri, from 17F.
# Ranks 3 and 4 are for dungeons of 50 floors or more: here they come
# only by ranking up (a monster that defeats another, Kron's wind).
