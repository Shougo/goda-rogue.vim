# The items. 種類 is what the item is, and decides what it can do:
# 武器 盾 腕輪 矢 草 巻物 杖 壺 食料 お金.
# 強さ is the strength of a weapon (attack) or a shield (defense), and
# how much the effect of another item does.
# 効果 is what an item does (- for nothing), by its kind:
#   腕輪, while it is worn:
#     ちから    adds 強さ to the strength
#     回復      restores HP twice as fast (and makes hungry twice as fast)
#     気配察知  shows the monsters within 強さ tiles (0: of the floor)
#     道具感知  shows the items of the floor
#   草, drunk:
#     HP回復    restores 強さ HP, or raises the max HP if it is full
#     ちから    adds 強さ to the strength
#   巻物, read:
#     あかり    lights the floor: its map, items and monsters
#     識別      identifies an item (nothing to identify yet)
#   杖, waved (a magic bolt to the front):
#     吹き飛ばし  blows the monster it hits 10 tiles away
#   食料, eaten:
#     満腹      restores 強さ of fullness
# ひび is how easily a bracelet cracks (a percent, 0 for never): leaving
# a floor where it had its effect, it cracks with ひび x (cracks + 1) %
# (times 腕輪の傷みやすさ in rules.txt), and breaks with too many cracks
# (ひびの段階 in rules.txt).
#   矢: 強さ is the attack of an arrow, shot or thrown
#   壺, things put in it:
#     保存      keeps them, to take out
#     合成      merges them: a weapon or a shield with one of its kind
#               (in the pot first) gets its seals and upgrade value, a
#               bracelet with the same bracelet is mended
# 回数 is how many times a staff can be waved, how many arrows there are,
# how many items a pot holds (min-max; - for others).
# 印 is the seal a weapon or a shield is born with (seals.txt; - for
# none), 印数 how many seals it can have. On another item, 印 makes it a
# material: merged into a weapon or a shield by mixed synthesis (異種合成,
# by a monster; a pot of synthesis merges items of a kind only), it adds
# its seal for weapons or for shields, and is used up.
# Any item can be thrown: an arrow hurts the monster it hits, a grass
# has its effect on it, another item hurts it a little.
# 値段 is the price in shops (of one arrow for arrows); a shop buys for
# half of it.
