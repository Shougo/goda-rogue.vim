# The items. 種類 is what the item is, and decides what it can do:
# 武器 盾 腕輪 矢 草 巻物 杖 壺 食料 お金.
# 強さ is the strength of a weapon (attack) or a shield (defense), and
# how much the effect of another item does.
# 効果 is what an item does (- for nothing), by its kind:
#   腕輪, while it is worn:
#     ちから    adds 強さ to the strength
#     回復      restores HP twice as fast (and makes hungry twice as fast)
#     気配察知  shows the monsters within 強さ tiles (0: of the floor)
#     道具感知  shows the items of the floor
#     サビよけ  nothing rusts (the seal does it by chance only)
#     盗みよけ  nothing is stolen nor ruined (the seal: by chance)
#     睡眠よけ  nothing puts the player to sleep
#     混乱よけ  nothing confuses the player
#     呪いよけ  nothing carried is cursed (a trap, a monster)
#     目つぶしよけ  nothing blinds the player
#     口封じよけ  nothing seals the mouth of the player
#     弱りよけ  nothing takes its attack or defense down
#     大食い  makes hungry twice as fast, and the blows of the player
#               do 強さ % more
#     垂れ流し  the monsters killed drop an item 強さ % more often; a
#               turn in ten, an item carried (not equipped) falls at the
#               player's feet
#     罠使い      shows every trap of the floor; the player never sets one
#               off by walking (stepping on one on purpose does), and
#               the monsters do: a pit takes one away, a mine halves its
#               HP, an arrow hurts it, a snare or sleep stops it for 強さ
#               of the trap turns, a warp sends it away
#     胃縮小    the blows of the monsters do 強さ % less damage to the
#               player (not under 1), which holds 25 less fullness
#     魔物呼び  the monsters of the floor come for the player, and new
#               ones come twice as often
#     転移    a turn in a hundred 強さ, the player is sent somewhere on
#               the floor
#     爆発      a turn in a hundred 強さ, the player blows up: its HP
#               halves, the monsters next to it are gone (no EXP), an
#               ally's or a shopkeeper's HP halves (an attack on a
#               shopkeeper), the items around it are gone, the walls
#               next to it crumble
#     耐爆      an explosion (a monster's, a mine) does half its damage
#               to the player
#     浮遊      the player floats over the waterways and the traps (it
#               sets none off by walking); rare
#     壁抜け    the player walks in the rock (not the edge of the floor),
#               losing 1 HP a turn there (never the last); taken off
#               there, the player is pushed out somewhere on the floor
#     呪い力    each cursed item carried (not in pots) adds 強さ to the
#               strength
#     空腹よけ  the monsters that suck the fullness (飢え) fail, and
#               the traps of hunger (腹減り)
#     遠投      what the player throws or shoots flies through the
#               monsters it hits (what it does, it does to each)
#     しあわせ  the EXP of the defeats is 強さ % more
#     忍び足    the monsters asleep in a room do not wake as the player
#               comes and goes (but in a monster house)
#     胃拡張    the player holds 強さ more fullness
#     値切り    the goods of shops cost 強さ % less (their prices tell
#               the kinds less well)
#     水渡り    the player walks on the waterways; taken off there, the
#               player falls in and is washed away somewhere on the floor
#     投げ下手  what the player throws or shoots never hits: it falls at
#               the feet of the monster it would have hit, and a trap
#               there goes off on it (as with 罠使い)
#     痛恨      強さ % of the blows of the monsters on the player are
#               critical, for 150% of the damage (a shield of 防恨 makes
#               them weaker)
#   A bracelet with a 印 (and no 効果) has its ability while worn, at
#   the strength an item is born with (seals.txt 強さ).
#   草, drunk:
#     HP回復    restores 強さ HP, or raises the max HP if it is full
#     ちから    under the max strength, restores 強さ of the strength
#               (not above the max); at it, adds 強さ to both
#     目薬      shows the traps of the floor, and the mimics hidden as
#               items that a sensing bracelet would show; cures
#               blindness
#     最大HP    raises the max HP by 強さ
#     最大満腹度+  raises the max fullness by 強さ (200 at most)
#     最大満腹度-  lowers it by 強さ (50 at least)
#     復活      carried (not in a pot), brings the player back once with
#               full HP when it falls (not from the wind); drunk, nothing
#     火炎      the player breathes fire to its front: the first monster
#               on the line takes 強さ (+-5) damage, defense aside; thrown,
#               half of it (at the player: its onigiri grill)
#   巻物, read:
#     あかり    lights the floor: its map, items and monsters
#     干上がり    the waterways of the floor dry up into floor
#     識別      identifies an item (nothing to identify yet)
#     武器強化  raises the upgrade value of the equipped weapon by 強さ
#     盾強化    raises the upgrade value of the equipped shield by 強さ
#               (both take the curse of what they upgrade away)
#     おはらい  takes the curses of everything carried away
#     印増大    adds a slot for seals to a weapon or a shield chosen
#               (10 at most)
#     印消し    takes a seal chosen away (not the ability an item is
#               born with)
#     斬撃  does 強さ damage to the enemies of the room (next to the
#               player, in a corridor)
#     全滅      the enemies of the room die (not a shopkeeper)
#     罠消し    takes every trap of the floor away
#     千里眼    shows the monsters of the floor while the player is on it
#     道具寄せ  brings the items of the floor (not the goods of a shop) to
#               the tiles around the player
#     迷子      sends the player somewhere on the floor
#     おにぎり  turns an item chosen (not equipped) into an onigiri
#     壺増大    a pot chosen holds one more
#     吸い出し  takes out everything in a pot chosen, around the player
#     祝福      blesses an item chosen (takes the curse of a cursed one
#               away instead)
#     錆止め    a weapon or a shield chosen (with room, no サビよけ yet)
#               gets the seal of サビよけ (錆)
#     引き寄せ  brings the enemies of the floor (not a shopkeeper) around
#               the player
#     救い  the HP back in full, and one of the troubles at random
#               solved (surrounded: those next bound; hungry: full;
#               cursed gear: uncursed; bad states: gone; strength down:
#               back)
#     パワーアップ  強さ turns of blows of 1.5 times the damage
#     予防      the bad states pass, and none comes for the floor
#     金縛り    the enemies next to the player are bound (かなしばり)
#     同士討ち  for 強さ turns, the enemies next to the player, about to
#               attack it, attack the other way, and use no ability
#     大部屋    the floor is one big room; the shopkeepers come by the
#               stairs (on them, while the player owes)
#     魔物部屋  the room of the player becomes a monster house (its
#               monsters asleep, items, no traps)
#     呪い      curses 強さ items carried at random
#     罠        traps (seen) on the tiles around the player
#     手ぶら    nothing is picked up for the floor
#     口封じ  the mouth of the player is sealed (口封じ)
#     聖域      read, nothing; put down, it sticks, and the enemies do
#               not attack the player on it
#     白紙      read, the player writes the name of a scroll it knows
#               (typed, with completion), and reads it as that scroll
#   杖, waved (a magic bolt to the front):
#     吹き飛ばし  blows the monster it hits 10 tiles away
#     仲間      makes the monster it hits an ally (not a shopkeeper or a
#               guard, and not past rules.txt 仲間の上限)
#     入れ替え  the player and the monster it hits change places
#     トンネル  digs 強さ tiles of rock to the front into a corridor (not
#               the edge of the floor nor a shop; it stops at a creature)
#   草, 巻物 and 杖 can also have an effect of the bolts of monsters
#   (monsters.txt 魔法: ワープ 倍速 混乱 鈍足 睡眠 封印 HP1/2 HP1/4 レベル-1
#   レベル-3 目つぶし かなしばり レベル+1 無敵 毒): a grass on who drinks it,
#   or on the monster it is thrown at; a scroll on the enemies of the room
#   (next to the player, in a corridor); a staff on the monster its bolt
#   hits
#   食料, eaten:
#     満腹      restores 強さ of fullness (an onigiri: it may rot)
#     腐った    restores 強さ of fullness, and brings confusion, slowness
#               or sleep (the rotten onigiri; mud and the trap of rot
#               make it)
#     焼き      restores 強さ of fullness and of HP, and never rots (the
#               grilled onigiri: a blast makes it of an onigiri carried)
# ひび is how easily a bracelet cracks (a percent, 0 for never): leaving
# a floor with it worn, it cracks with ひび x (cracks + 1) % (times
# 腕輪の傷みやすさ in rules.txt, and twice that with two bracelets worn),
# and breaks with too many cracks (ひびの段階 in rules.txt).
#   矢: 強さ is the attack of an arrow, shot or thrown
#   壺, things put in it:
#     保存      keeps them, to take out
#     合成      merges them: a weapon or a shield with one of its kind
#               (in the pot first) gets its seals and upgrade value, a
#               bracelet with the same bracelet is mended
#     全回復    restores all HP for each thing put in, which is gone
#               with its room in the pot
#     普通      keeps them, not to use nor take out: they come out when
#               the pot breaks (a crow makes one of another pot; on the
#               floors of the hard dungeons only)
#     底抜け    what is put in is gone, with its room; broken, it leaves
#               a pit where it breaks
#   (these keep what is put in, to take out, changed:)
#     鑑定      identified (its upgrade value or charges, and its curse)
#     強化      a weapon or a shield: its upgrade value up by 強さ
#     弱化      a weapon or a shield: its upgrade value down by 強さ
#     換金      money: what a shop would pay for it
#     変化      another item, of the item table 変化 (item_tables.txt)
# 回数 is how many times a staff can be waved, how many arrows there are,
# how many items a pot holds (min-max; - for others).
# 印 is the ability a weapon, a shield or a bracelet is born with (素の能力: a seal
# of seals.txt at its strength 強さ; - for none): it takes no slot, and
# stays when the item is the base of a synthesis; merged into another,
# it becomes a seal of that one, at 印の強さ. 印数 is how many seals it
# can have. On another item, 印 makes it a material: merged into a weapon
# or a shield by mixed synthesis (異種合成, by a monster; a pot of
# synthesis merges items of a kind only), it adds its seal for weapons or
# for shields, and is used up.
# Any item can be thrown: an arrow hurts the monster it hits, a grass
# has its effect on it, another item hurts it a little.
# 値段 is the price in shops (of one arrow for arrows); a shop buys for
# half of it.
