# The monsters. One family is one glyph (字); one kind is one rank.
# 速度: 鈍足 (every other turn), 通常, 倍速 (twice a turn), 移動倍速
# (twice a turn, but one attack at most).
# 能力: special abilities, separated by spaces, each with its numbers
# after colons (- for none):
#   怒り:H:A   at H% of its max HP or below, its attack becomes A% (once)
#   痛恨:C:D   C% of its blows are critical, doing D% of the damage
#   ふらふら:C  C% of its actions are a step in a random direction
#   突進:R:D   with the player on one of its lines, 2 to R tiles away:
#              it rushes up, does D damage to the first creature in the
#              way and blows it away (as the staff)
#   引き寄せ:R:C:H  with the player on one of its lines, 2 to R tiles
#              away (R 0: anywhere in its room), C% of its actions pull
#              the player next to it, and then it hits (H 1) or not (0)
#   射撃:R:C:A:P  with the player in sight on one of its lines, 2 to R
#              tiles away, C% of its actions shoot the arrow A (an item's
#              ID) at it, even past a creature in the way: it hits the
#              first one it meets (at rules.txt 敵の投擲命中), or every
#              one with P 1 (and then never falls)
#   魔法:R:C:E  with the player in sight on one of its lines, 2 to R tiles
#              away, C% of its actions wave a staff at it: a bolt of one
#              of the effects E (separated by commas), at random, to the
#              first creature on the way: ワープ, 倍速 (the player for 20
#              turns, a monster for good), 吹き飛ばし, 混乱 (10 turns:
#              moving and attacking at random), 鈍足 (10 actions of two
#              turns), 睡眠 (5 turns), 封印 (the player: no eating,
#              drinking, reading or talking on the floor; a monster: no
#              abilities), HP1/2, HP1/4, レベル-1, レベル-3 (a monster:
#              ranks), 目つぶし (10 turns: the player sees only next to
#              it, a monster walks at random), かなしばり (an enemy until a
#              blow; the player and allies until they lose HP, or for 20
#              turns), レベル+1, 無敵 (20 turns: no damage), 毒 (5 damage,
#              and a point of strength; a monster: half its attack, and slowed)
#   分裂:C     a blow of the player that leaves it with 2 HP or more
#              splits it C% of the time: its HP halves, and another with
#              that HP appears next to it (it gives nothing when defeated)
#   腐らせる:C:N:X  next to the player, C% of its blows rot N of the
#              onigiri carried instead (not those in pots); with none
#              and X 1, an item that is no equipment turns into a rotten
#              onigiri
#   盗み:C:W:N:E  next to the player, C% of its blows are thefts instead:
#              W 0 as much money as a pile on the floor (at most the
#              purse), W 1 N carried items (with E 1, equipped ones too);
#              then it warps away and keeps away from the player, never
#              stealing again. Defeated, it drops what it stole, not its
#              落とし物
#   合成:N:C   it swallows the items the player throws at it, N of them,
#              and merges them as a pot of synthesis does, and also a
#              material into a weapon or a shield swallowed before it
#              (異種合成: the seal of the material, items.txt 印); with C
#              above 0, next to the player, C% of its blows suck a weapon
#              or a shield the player carries in instead, an equipped one
#              too (they count in N). Defeated, it drops what it holds
#   呪い:C:W:N  next to the player, C% of its blows curse N of the items
#              carried instead: W 0 the weapon or the shield equipped, W 1
#              those or the bracelet equipped, W 2 any item, equipped or
#              not. With nothing to curse, it attacks. A scroll of
#              おはらい read in its room (or next to it) destroys it
#   飢え:C:N:M  next to the player, C% of its blows take N of its
#              fullness instead, or (M above 0) M of its max fullness
#              (never under a fifth of 満腹度; eating more than the
#              player can hold brings 5 back). Food thrown at it
#              defeats it
#   不動  it never moves by itself (a staff or a blow may move it)
#   捕縛:C:T  a blow of it that hits holds the player C% of the time: it
#              cannot step away for T turns, or until the monster is gone
#              or no more next to it
#   燃える  what is thrown or shot at it burns, with no effect: only
#              blows hurt it
#   回復:C:N  C% of its actions heal another hurt monster it sees by N HP
#   鼓舞:C  C% of its actions rank up another monster of the floors it
#              sees (not one that cheers)
#   復活:P  defeated the first time, it gets up again with P% of its
#              max HP (and gives nothing yet)
#   爆発:H:D  hurt down to H% of its max HP or below, it explodes at its
#              next action: every creature next to it loses D% of its HP,
#              the items next to it and under it burn, and it is gone,
#              giving nothing
#   群れ:N  it comes with N more of its kind next to it, on a new floor
#              and when it appears later
#   逃げる  it keeps away from the player it sees, and fights only when
#              it cannot; the other monsters that see it hunt it
#              before the player, and rank up by killing it
#   罠:C  C% of its actions, out of the player's reach, hide a trap
#              of the floor's table where it stands; defeated, it leaves
#              one there
#   長柄  it hits the player two tiles away on one of its lines, with
#              nothing in between
#   瞬間移動:C  with the player in sight and not next to it, C% of its
#              actions take it next to the player
#   桂馬:D  it moves as a knight of shogi or chess, over what is in the
#              way; with the player a knight's move away, it swoops on it
#              for D damage, which defense does not lower
#   透明  the player does not see it, unless eye drops cleared its
#              eyes on the floor
#   壁抜け  it goes through rock toward the player, wherever it is; it
#              fights only out of rock
#   投げつけ:R:C:A  standing on an item, with the player on one of its
#              lines within R tiles (with A 1, anywhere within them in
#              sight), C% of its actions throw the item at the player (a
#              grass has its effect on the player); it goes for the items
#              it sees before the player
#   反射:C:W  C% of the bolts of staffs waved at it go back to the
#              player; with W 1 the arrows too, with W 2 any item thrown
#              (it falls at the player's feet)
#   荒らし:C:W  next to the player, C% of its actions ruin an item carried
#              instead of a blow: W 0 empties a staff, 1 takes a grass or
#              a scroll away, 2 makes a pot a plain pot (普通の壺), 3 any of
#              those; the seal 盗みよけ wards it off
#   サビ:C:N:H  it never attacks: next to the player, C% of its actions
#              rust what the player has equipped by N (H 0: the weapon or
#              the shield, 1: both, 2: one, or half of the time a seal of
#              one taken away), the others watch; a seal of サビよけ is
#              taken away first, one born with it makes it fail
#   状態:C:S:W:T  C% of its actions bring a state to the player for T turns
#              (W 0: blind, sees only next to it; 1: asleep; 2: confused;
#              3: its attack or its defense down, at random, 30%, longer
#              by T if down already, until the next floor; 4: its mouth
#              sealed for the floor, T unused): next to it
#              instead of a blow, or with S 1 from anywhere it sees the
#              player; the bracelets of 睡眠よけ and 混乱よけ ward off sleep and
#              confusion
#   擬態:D:S:K  on a new floor it hides as an item of the floor's table, or
#              (S%) as stairs, or (K%, with a shop) as one of the shop's
#              goods. D 1: trying to pick it up shows it, and so do a
#              sensing bracelet in reach and eye drops (目薬). D 2: it is
#              picked up and carried; using it in any way (throwing and
#              putting down too) shows it, and so does the next floor;
#              identified, it is listed as the monster. Shown, it appears
#              next to the player and strikes first; defeated, what it
#              drops is the item it looked like
# 眠り: the share asleep on a new floor, a percent (- for half).
# 落とし物: what it drops when the player kills it, and how often: 道具:P
# (P% an item of the floor's table), お金:P, a kind of items:P (矢:P, an
# arrow of the floor's table), an item's ID:P, なし, or - (as rules.txt
# 落とし物 says).
# A monster of the floors that defeats another ranks up.
# 説明: what the maker says of it, shown under what its abilities do when
# the player looks at it (- for none).
# 属性: what it is, separated by spaces: 水棲 (it swims) or 浮遊 (it
# floats), both of which go over the waterways; ドラゴン, 不死, 鬼族,
# 金属 or 植物, the kinds the seals of slaying go for (seals.txt 対象);
# - for none.
# 種別: - for monsters of the floors, 店主 for shopkeepers, 番犬 for the
# guards that come after a thief (shops.txt); these two never appear on
# the floors by themselves, nor rank up.
