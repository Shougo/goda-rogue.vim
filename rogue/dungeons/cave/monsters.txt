# The monsters. One family is one glyph (字); one kind is one rank.
# 速度: 鈍足 (every other turn), 通常, 倍速 (twice a turn), 移動倍速
# (twice a turn, but one attack at most).
# 能力: special abilities, separated by spaces, each with its numbers
# after colons (- for none):
#   怒り:H:A   at H% of its max HP or below, its attack becomes A% (once)
#   痛恨:C:D   C% of its blows are critical, doing D% of the damage
#   ふらふら:C  C% of its actions are a step in a random direction
#   突進:R:D   with the player on one of its lines, 2 to R tiles away:
#              it rushes up, does D damage to the first creature in the
#              way and blows it away (as the staff)
#   引き寄せ:R:C:H  with the player on one of its lines, 2 to R tiles
#              away (R 0: anywhere in its room), C% of its actions pull
#              the player next to it, and then it hits (H 1) or not (0)
#   射撃:R:C:A:P  with the player in sight on one of its lines, 2 to R
#              tiles away, C% of its actions shoot the arrow A (an item's
#              ID) at it, even past a creature in the way: it hits the
#              first one it meets (at rules.txt 敵の投擲命中), or every
#              one with P 1 (and then never falls)
#   魔法:R:C:E  with the player in sight on one of its lines, 2 to R tiles
#              away, C% of its actions wave a staff at it: a bolt of one
#              of the effects E (separated by commas), at random, to the
#              first creature on the way: ワープ, 倍速 (the player for 20
#              turns, a monster for good), 吹き飛ばし, 混乱 (10 turns:
#              moving and attacking at random), 鈍足 (10 actions of two
#              turns), 睡眠 (5 turns), 封印 (the player: no eating,
#              drinking, reading or talking on the floor; a monster: no
#              abilities), HP1/2, HP1/4, レベル-1, レベル-3 (a monster:
#              ranks)
# 眠り: the share asleep on a new floor, a percent (- for a third).
# 落とし物: what it drops when the player kills it, and how often: 道具:P
# (P% an item of the floor's table), お金:P, a kind of items:P (矢:P, an
# arrow of the floor's table), an item's ID:P, なし, or - (as rules.txt
# 落とし物 says).
# A monster of the floors that defeats another ranks up.
# 説明: what the maker says of it, shown under what its abilities do when
# the player looks at it (- for none).
# 種別: - for monsters of the floors, 店主 for shopkeepers, 番犬 for the
# guards that come after a thief (shops.txt); these two never appear on
# the floors by themselves, nor rank up.
