# The traps. 効果 is what a trap does when the player steps on it, with
# 強さ as how much:
#   落とし穴  the player falls to the next floor, with 強さ damage
#   地雷      halves the HP of the player
#   大型地雷  leaves a quarter of the HP of the player
#   トラばさみ  the player cannot move for 強さ turns
#   毒矢      強さ damage, and the strength goes down by 1
#   睡眠      the player sleeps for 強さ turns
#   ワープ    the player is sent somewhere on the floor
#   腹減り    the fullness goes down by 強さ
#   サビ      the upgrade values of the weapon and the shield go down by 1
#   腐り      every onigiri the player carries rots (not those in pots)
#   呪い      強さ of the items carried that are no equipment, at random,
#             are cursed
